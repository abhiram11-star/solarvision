UNDERSTANDING THE PHENOMENON

Actual vs Apparent Position

Why does the Sun appear at a different position from where it actually is?

01. Actual Position of the Sun

The actual position is the real position of the Sun in the sky before considering the bending of light due to Earth's atmosphere.

02. Apparent Position of the Sun

The apparent position is the position where the Sun appears to be when we observe it from Earth. Due to atmospheric refraction, the Sun appears higher than its actual position near the horizon.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SUN

How Does It Happen?

Sunlight travels through different layers of Earth's atmosphere before reaching our eyes.

01. Light Enters the Atmosphere

Sunlight enters Earth's atmosphere from space and passes through layers of air having different densities.

02. Light Changes Direction

As light travels through these layers, it continuously bends due to atmospheric refraction.

03. We See the Apparent Position

Our eyes trace the refracted light backwards in a straight line, making the Sun appear higher than its actual position near the horizon.

VISUALISING ATMOSPHERIC REFRACTION

Where Is the Sun Actually?

Due to atmospheric refraction, the Sun appears higher in the sky than its actual position.

Understanding the Two Positions

Atmospheric Refraction of Sunlight

Light from the Sun bends as it passes through Earth's atmosphere. The observer sees the Sun at a higher apparent position.

UNDERSTANDING THE SCIENCE

Why Does the Sun Appear Higher?

Atmospheric Refraction Explained

Earth's atmosphere consists of layers of air with different optical densities. Generally, the optical density increases closer to Earth's surface.

When sunlight passes through these layers, it continuously bends towards the normal as it enters optically denser layers.

Our eyes see the refracted sunlight and trace it backwards in a straight line. This makes the Sun appear higher than its actual position.
